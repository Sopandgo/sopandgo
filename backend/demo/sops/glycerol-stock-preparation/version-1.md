# Bacterial Glycerol Stock Preparation (Demo)

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how to prepare glycerol stocks for long-term storage of bacterial strains.

## 2. Scope

Non-pathogenic *E. coli* laboratory strains.

**Disclaimer: This is a demonstrational document only and should not be used for actual laboratory work without verification by qualified personnel.**

## 3. Materials

1. Overnight culture in LB medium
2. Sterile 50 % glycerol
3. Sterile cryovials with screw caps
4. Freezer at −80 °C

## 4. Procedure

1. **Grow:** Inoculate 5 mL LB with a single colony and incubate overnight at 37 °C with shaking.
2. **Mix:** Combine 500 µL of the culture with 500 µL of 50 % glycerol in a cryovial (final 25 % glycerol).
3. **Label:** Write the strain name, plasmid, date, and your initials on the vial.
4. **Freeze:** Store the vial at −80 °C.
