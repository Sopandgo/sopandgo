# Pipette Performance Check (Demo)

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes a routine gravimetric check of air-displacement pipettes between calibrations.

## 2. Scope

Single-channel adjustable pipettes (P10 to P1000) used in the lab.

**Disclaimer: This is a demonstrational document only and should not be used for actual laboratory work without verification by qualified personnel.**

## 3. Responsibilities

* Each user checks their assigned pipettes once per month.
* The lab manager reviews the log and arranges service for failed pipettes.

## 4. Materials and Equipment

1. Analytical balance (readability 0.1 mg or better)
2. Weighing vessel with lid
3. Deionized water at room temperature
4. Thermometer
5. Matching pipette tips

## 5. Procedure

1. **Equilibrate:** Keep water, pipette, and tips in the room for at least 1 hour. Record the water temperature.
2. **Tare:** Place the weighing vessel on the balance and tare it.
3. **Pre-wet:** Aspirate and dispense the test volume three times to wet the tip.
4. **Weigh:** Dispense the nominal volume into the vessel and record the mass. Repeat for 10 readings.
5. **Calculate:** Convert mass to volume with the density of water at the recorded temperature.
6. **Assess:** Compare the mean volume (accuracy) and standard deviation (precision) to the limits in Table 1.

| Nominal volume | Accuracy limit | Precision limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 µL | ± 1.0 % | ≤ 0.8 % |
| 100 µL | ± 0.8 % | ≤ 0.3 % |
| 1000 µL | ± 0.8 % | ≤ 0.3 % |

*Table 1: Example acceptance limits for this demo.*

## 6. Results

* **Pass:** Record the result in the pipette log and attach a dated check label.
* **Fail:** Label the pipette "Do not use", remove it from service, and inform the lab manager.

## 7. Records

Keep the pipette log for at least three years.
