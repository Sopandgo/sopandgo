# Chemical Spill Response (Demo)

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how to respond to a chemical spill in the lab.

## 2. Scope

Spills of liquid or solid chemicals inside the lab rooms.

**Disclaimer: This is a demonstrational document only and should not be used for actual laboratory work without verification by qualified personnel.**

## 3. Responsibilities

* Everyone working in the lab must know where the spill kit is.
* The lab manager restocks the spill kit after each use.

## 4. Spill Kit Contents

1. Absorbent pads and granules
2. Nitrile gloves and safety goggles
3. Dustpan and scoop
4. Heavy-duty waste bags and labels

## 5. Procedure

1. **Alert:** Tell people nearby about the spill.
2. **Protect:** Put on gloves and goggles before you approach the spill.
3. **Contain:** Surround the spill with absorbent to stop it spreading.
4. **Absorb:** Cover the spill with absorbent and wait until the liquid is taken up.
5. **Collect:** Scoop the material into a waste bag and seal it.
6. **Clean:** Wipe the area with water and detergent.
7. **Dispose:** Label the bag with the chemical name and hand it in as chemical waste.

## 6. After the Spill

Tell the lab manager so the spill kit can be restocked.
