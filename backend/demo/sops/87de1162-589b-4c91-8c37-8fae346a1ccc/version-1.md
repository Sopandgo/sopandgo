# Agarose Gel Electrophoresis (Demo)

## 1. Purpose

This protocol describes how to separate DNA/RNA by size using a horizontal gel.

## 2. Scope

Analysis of PCR products and genomic DNA.

**Disclaimer: This is a demonstrational document only and should not be used for actual laboratory work without verification by qualified personnel.**

## 3. Responsibilities

* Personnel must be trained in lab safety.
* Management must ensure equipment is available.

## 4. Safety Precautions

| Hazard | Source | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Electrical** | Power Supply | Do not touch the tank while the run is active. |
| **Chemical** | Ethidium Bromide | Wear gloves. EtBr is a mutagen. |
| **Radiation** | UV Light | Wear goggles when looking at the transilluminator. |

## 5. Materials and Equipment

### 5.1 Equipment

1. Electrophoresis Tank
2. Power Supply
3. Casting Tray and Combs
4. Microwave
5. UV Box (Transilluminator)
6. Pipettes

### 5.2 Reagents

1. Agarose
2. Running Buffer (TAE)
3. 6X Loading Dye
4. Ethidium Bromide (EtBr)
5. DNA Ladder

## 6. Procedure

### 6.1 Gel Preparation

1. **Weigh Agarose:** Add 1.0g of agarose to 100mL of buffer for a 1% gel.
2. **Melt:** Heat in microwave until clear. Use caution as the glass will be hot.
3. **Cool:** Let the flask cool until it is comfortable to touch.
4. **Stain:** Add EtBr to the molten agarose (approx 5 µL).
5. **Pour:** Place the comb in the tray and pour the gel. Wait for it to harden.

### 6.2 Sample Loading and Running

1. **Setup:** Place the gel in the tank and fill with TAE buffer until covered.
2. **Prepare Samples:** Mix DNA with loading dye on a piece of parafilm or in a tube.
3. **Load:** Carefully pipette samples into the wells.

* **Reference:** Figure 1 shows the application of a PCR product.

![PCR reaction product being applied to an agarose gel. (c) Karolina Fok / Wikimedia Commons / CC BY 4.0](assets/agarose-gel-application.jpg)

4. **Run:** Set the power supply to 100V. Run for 45 minutes or until the dye reaches the end.

### 6.3 Visualization

1. Remove the gel from the tank and place it on the UV transilluminator.
2. Turn on the UV light to see the bands.
3. Record the results in the lab notebook.

## 7. Waste Disposal

Dispose of gels in the hazardous waste bin.
